The Coffee Ledgerof Melbourne, kept in black

Melbourne's most-awarded coffee spots, scored out of 10 from The Age Good Food Guide, the World's 100 Best Coffee Shops list, barista competition titles and the city's major food guides. Each score shows exactly which listings earned it.

    How the score works

    Each listing adds points. The raw total is scaled so 8 points equals 10/10, capped at 10. Awards from judged panels weigh more than editorial roundups.

    Good Food Guide cafe awards are judged on the whole cafe (food, service, room), not coffee alone. Only Coffee Project's world ranking belongs to its Sydney flagship, so its Melbourne Quarter outpost gets a reduced affiliate credit. Scores reflect published recognition, not our own tasting.
